## Status

Of eight required calculation packages, **zero are fully closed**; five (P01-C01, P01-C02, P01-C04, P01-C07, P01-C08) are partially structured via existing reproducible scripts (`power_budget_calc.py`, `energy_autonomy_calc.py`), and three (P01-C03, P01-C05, P01-C06) remain `OPEN` — though as of batch 7, P01-C05/C06's blockers are now named and specific (DEC-03's architecture conflict; PV-01's physical bench test) rather than simply unstarted. This mirrors `06_decisions/decision_register.md`'s finding across batches 5–7: GATE-2's blocking list widened (batch 5), had its numeric envelope narrowed without closing (batch 6), then reshaped again (batch 7) — several items closer to done, one (DEC-03) newly and more sharply blocked. Per `docs/00_shared/engineering_rules.md` #4, any future work on these packages should extend the existing dataclass/function pattern in `power_budget_calc.py`/`energy_autonomy_calc.py` (or a sibling script) rather than introducing manually-typed totals — batch 6 itself is a live example of why: a manually-typed total in the source document (≈3.32 mA) did not match its own stated formula (≈3.04 mA) and was caught only by re-running the calculation.
