**2026-09-17 status (post GATE-2 batch-3 closure pass, per the 17.09.2026 REFERENCE_BASELINE):** GAP-02, GAP-03 and GAP-08 closed; SOL-009's pull-up value is now `CALCULATED` (closed, analytical). Closing those, however, surfaced findings more fundamental than the four original blockers: **SOL-001, SOL-002 and SOL-004 are reopened** (`OPEN`, not `IN_PROGRESS`) because MCP73871 has only one physical input (no internal USB/solar arbitration exists to rely on) and TPS7A02 cannot supply the ESP32's active-mode current alone. **SOL-006/SOL-013 carry a new, unresolved hardware-safety finding** (DEC-12): the current power-gating design would hold BME280's I2C pins logic-high while its VDDIO is off, which its datasheet forbids. **SOL-003 is scope-narrowed**: MCP73871 satisfies charge-regulation but not independent battery-side protection (DEC-13, new, OPEN). None of DEC-01, DEC-02, DEC-04, DEC-12 or DEC-13 are resolved as of this update — GATE-2 remains BLOCKED on them. SOL-012 is `NOT_APPLICABLE` as a candidate decision (DEC-08), not a closed one, since it depends on SOL-006's sensor selection remaining BME280.
